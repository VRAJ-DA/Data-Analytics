# Types of Data Analysis

There are four major types of Data Analysis.

## 1. Descriptive Analysis

### Question:

> What happened?

Descriptive Analysis summarizes historical data.

### Examples

- Total sales were ₹10,00,000.
- 5,000 orders were completed.
- The West region generated the highest revenue.

### Common Tools

- Excel
- SQL
- Power BI

---

## 2. Diagnostic Analysis

### Question:

> Why did it happen?

Diagnostic Analysis investigates the reasons behind a result.

### Example

Sales decreased because:

- Product demand decreased
- Prices increased
- Customer complaints increased
- Marketing activity decreased

---

## 3. Predictive Analysis

### Question:

> What is likely to happen?

Predictive Analysis uses historical data, trends, and statistical methods to forecast future outcomes.

### Examples

- Predicting future sales
- Forecasting customer demand
- Predicting customer churn

---

## 4. Prescriptive Analysis

### Question:

> What should we do?

Prescriptive Analysis recommends possible actions based on data.

### Example

If sales are decreasing, the business may:

- Offer targeted discounts
- Improve marketing
- Focus on high-performing products
- Target specific customer segments

## Summary

| Type | Main Question |
|---|---|
| Descriptive | What happened? |
| Diagnostic | Why did it happen? |
| Predictive | What may happen? |
| Prescriptive | What should we do? |
